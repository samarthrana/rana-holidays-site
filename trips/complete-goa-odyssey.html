Goa

The Complete Goa Odyssey

Old forts, spice farms, North and South Goa beaches

Day by day

    • Airport Pickup (Mopa GOX): Your private chauffeur will meet you at Mopa Airport arrivals with a name placard for a smooth transfer towards Panjim / North Goa.
    • Evening Mandovi River Sunset Cruise: Kick off your trip directly with a lively sunset cruise on the Mandovi River from Panjim. Enjoy live Goan music, traditional folk dances, scenic river views, and floating casinos.
    • Hotel Check-in (North Goa - 3 Nights Stay): After the cruise and dinner in Panjim, head to your North Goa hotel (Candolim/Calangute area) for check-in and your first night's stay.

    • Morning - Fort Aguada & Sinquerim: Visit the 17th-century Portuguese Fort Aguada and lighthouse with sweeping sea views.
    • Calangute & Baga Beach Hub: Explore Calangute and Baga beaches, vibrant shacks, and water sports activities.
    • Evening Nightlife: Experience the nightlife and beach clubs around Baga and Tito's Lane.

    • Morning - Chapora Fort & Vagator Beach: Hike up Chapora Fort ('Dil Chahta Hai' fort) and relax at Vagator / Ozran beach cliffs.
    • Afternoon Local Markets: Explore Anjuna Flea Market or local boutique shops for clothes, spices, and handicrafts.
    • Evening Sunset Dinner: Dine at a famous cliffside restaurant overlooking the Arabian Sea.

    • Check-out & Transfer South: Check out and drive toward South Goa.
    • Old Goa UNESCO Churches & Fontainhas: En route, stop at Old Goa to explore Basilica of Bom Jesus and Se Cathedral, followed by a walk through Panjim's colorful Latin Quarter (Fontainhas).
    • South Goa Check-in & Colva Beach (3 Nights Stay): Check into your South Goa hotel and spend the evening relaxing at Colva Beach.

    • Morning - Cabo de Rama Fort: Visit the dramatic cliffside Cabo de Rama Fort for breathtaking ocean panoramas.
    • Palolem Beach: Head to Palolem Beach, a picturesque crescent bay lined with palm trees. Enjoy kayaking or a short boat ride.
    • Evening Leisure: Dine at a beachside shack under the stars in South Goa.

    • Morning - Tropical Spice Plantation: Take a guided tour of a local spice farm (e.g., Sahakari Spice Farm) featuring aromatic trees, traditional Goan buffet lunch, and cultural welcome.
    • Afternoon - Margao Municipal Market: Explore Margao's bustling local market to shop for authentic Goan sausages, local spices, bebinca, and cashews.
    • Evening Relaxation: Unwind at your South Goa resort.

    • Morning Beach Walk & Breakfast: Enjoy a peaceful morning walk at Benaulim or Varca beach and a leisurely breakfast at the hotel.
    • Checkout & Airport Drop (Dabolim GOI): Complete hotel checkout. Your private chauffeur will drive you smoothly to Goa International Airport, Dabolim (GOI) for your departure flight.
