Ayodhya, Prayagraj & Varanasi

Ram Janmabhoomi, Triveni Sangam, Ganga Aarti

DaysStaysIncludedPrice

Day by day

    • Arrival: Transfer from Ayodhya Airport or Railway Station to your hotel.
    • Afternoon Exploration: Begin your journey at the sacred Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Temple, followed by visits to Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan, and Dashrath Mahal.
    • Evening: Stroll along Ram Ki Paidi and Saryu Ghat to witness the mesmerizing Saryu Aarti.

    Overnight Stay: Ayodhya

    • Morning Sightseeing: After breakfast, explore historical and spiritual sites including Nageshwarnath Temple, Sita Ki Rasoi, and Guptar Ghat.
    • Afternoon Visits: Discover Bharat Kund, the historic Mani Parvat, and Valmiki Bhawan.
    • Evening: Unwind with some local shopping or leisure time near the holy town.

    Overnight Stay: Ayodhya

    • Transit: Check out after breakfast and drive to Prayagraj (Allahabad).
    • Key Highlights: Take a boat ride to the holy Triveni Sangam (confluence of Ganga, Yamuna, and Saraswati). Visit Bade Hanuman Ji Temple, Alopi Devi Temple, Anand Bhawan, Akshay Vat, and view the historic Allahabad Fort.
    • Evening: Check in and relax at your hotel.

    Overnight Stay: Prayagraj

    • Transit: Enjoy breakfast, check out, and drive towards the spiritual capital, Varanasi.
    • Afternoon Sightseeing: Check into your hotel and proceed to the Kashi corridors. Visit the revered Kashi Vishwanath Temple, Annapurna Temple, and Vishalakshi Temple.
    • Evening: Head to Dashashwamedh Ghat to experience the world-famous, grand Ganga Aarti.

    Overnight Stay: Varanasi

    • Early Morning: Experience a serene boat ride on the River Ganga to witness the sunrise over the historic ghats.
    • Day Tour: Visit Kaal Bhairav Temple, Sankat Mochan, Durga Kund, and Banaras Hindu University (BHU).
    • Afternoon & Sarnath: Travel a short distance to Sarnath to explore the Dhamek Stupa and Mula Gandha Kuti Vihar.
    • Evening: Explore local Banarasi markets (famous for silk and sweets).

    Overnight Stay: Varanasi

    • Morning: Enjoy a leisurely breakfast and check out from the hotel.
    • Departure: Depending on your schedule, squeeze in some final shopping or sightseeing before being transferred to the Varanasi Airport or Railway Station for your onward journey.
