Rann of Kutch

The White Rann, Tent City, Bhuj palaces, Mandvi beach

  • Travel style: Couple
  • 2 hotel stays

  2. Bhuj2N

DaysStaysIncludedPrice

Day by day

  1. Upon arrival at Bhuj Airport, meet your driver and proceed to your hotel. Complete the check-in formalities and relax. Later, explore some of Bhuj's famous heritage attractions.

    Places to Visit:

    • Aina Mahal
    • Prag Mahal
    • Kutch Museum
    • Hamirsar Lake
    • Local Bhuj Market

    Return to the hotel after sightseeing and enjoy dinner.

    Meals: DinnerOvernight Stay: Bhuj

  2. After breakfast at the hotel, proceed for a full-day excursion to Mandvi, a beautiful coastal town known for its royal heritage and peaceful beaches.

    Places to Visit:

    • Vijay Vilas Palace
    • Mandvi Beach
    • Shyamji Krishna Varma Memorial
    • Traditional Shipbuilding Yard
    • Sunset at Mandvi Beach

    Spend some quality time together by the Arabian Sea before returning to Bhuj.

    Meals: Breakfast & DinnerOvernight Stay: Bhuj

  3. After breakfast, check out from the hotel and proceed towards Dhordo Tent City, located near the magnificent Great Rann of Kutch. En route, visit traditional Kutchi villages and experience the region's rich handicraft culture.

    Places to Visit & Activities:

    • Bhujodi Handicraft Village
    • Nirona Village (traditional Rogan art, subject to availability)
    • Dhordo Tent City check-in
    • White Rann of Kutch
    • Romantic sunset at the White Desert
    • Cultural performances and traditional activities, subject to camp schedule

    Enjoy the spectacular white salt desert, capture beautiful couple photographs, and experience the unique atmosphere of the Rann.

    Meals: Breakfast & DinnerOvernight Stay: Dhordo Tent City

  4. After breakfast at Tent City, check out and proceed towards Bhuj. Enjoy scenic views of the Kutch landscape and explore the region's famous attractions along the way.

    Places to Visit:

    • Kala Dungar (Black Hill)
    • Dattatreya Temple
    • India Bridge Viewpoint (subject to access restrictions)
    • Khavda Village
    • Kutch Handicraft Shopping

    Later, continue to Bhuj and check in at your hotel. Spend the evening at leisure.

    Meals: Breakfast & DinnerOvernight Stay: Bhuj

  5. After breakfast at the hotel, check out and proceed to Bhuj Airport for your onward journey. Depart with beautiful memories of the White Rann, traditional Kutchi culture, and a romantic desert holiday.

    Meals: Breakfast
