Day by day

  1. Welcome to Kuala Lumpur!

    Upon arrival at Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA), meet our representative and proceed to the hotel for check-in.

    Spend the rest of the day at leisure, relaxing at the hotel or exploring the surroundings at your own pace.

    Overnight Stay: Kuala Lumpur

  2. Enjoy breakfast at the hotel.

    Today, proceed for a Kuala Lumpur City Tour covering the city's iconic landmarks and cultural attractions.

    Sightseeing Highlights:

    • Petronas Twin Towers – Photo Stop
    • KL Tower
    • King's Palace
    • National Museum
    • Kwai Chai Hong
    • Sultan Abdul Samad Building
    • Independence Square (Dataran Merdeka)
    • Local Chocolate Boutique

    After completing the city tour, return to the hotel.

    Overnight Stay: Kuala Lumpur

  3. Enjoy breakfast at the hotel.

    Proceed for a full-day excursion to Genting Highlands, a popular mountain resort surrounded by lush greenery and scenic landscapes.

    En route, visit the famous Batu Caves, known for its impressive limestone caves and the iconic Lord Murugan statue.

    Continue to Genting Highlands and enjoy a two-way cable car ride in a standard gondola, offering beautiful panoramic views of the surrounding mountains and rainforest.

    After the tour, return to Kuala Lumpur.

    Overnight Stay: Kuala Lumpur

  4. Enjoy breakfast at the hotel.

    Today, proceed for a fun-filled day at Sunway Lagoon, one of Malaysia's most popular multi-park entertainment destinations.

    Enjoy access to the Six Parks, offering a variety of exciting rides, water attractions, adventure activities and entertainment suitable for visitors of all ages.

    Spend the day enjoying the attractions and activities at Sunway Lagoon.

    Return to the hotel after the tour.

    Overnight Stay: Kuala Lumpur

  5. Enjoy breakfast at the hotel.

    Check out from the hotel by 1200 HRS.

    Proceed to Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) for your onward flight.

    TOUR ENDS WITH BEAUTIFUL MEMORIES OF KUALA LUMPUR!
