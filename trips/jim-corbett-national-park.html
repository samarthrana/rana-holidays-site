Jim Corbett National Park Tour

Jungle safari, Garjiya Devi, Kosi river views

Day by day

  1. Begin your journey from Delhi in the morning and proceed towards Jim Corbett National Park, situated in the scenic foothills of the Himalayas.

    The journey takes approximately 6–7 hours, depending on traffic and road conditions. Enjoy the changing landscapes as you travel through the countryside of Uttar Pradesh and enter the picturesque surroundings of Uttarakhand.

    Upon arrival in the Corbett/Ramnagar area, proceed to your selected wildlife resort and complete the check-in formalities.

    Take some time to relax and unwind amidst the peaceful natural surroundings of the resort.

    In the afternoon/evening, proceed to visit:

    Garjiya Devi Temple

    Visit the famous Garjiya Devi Temple, beautifully situated on a large rock formation in the Kosi River. The temple is one of the prominent attractions around Corbett and offers scenic views of the river and surrounding forested landscape.

    After the visit, return to your resort.

    Evening at Leisure

    Spend the evening relaxing at the resort and enjoying its facilities and natural surroundings.

    Optional activities may include:

    • Nature walks around the resort
    • Birdwatching
    • Bonfire, subject to resort availability
    • Leisure time at the swimming pool
    • Other resort-based recreational activities

    Overnight Stay: Corbett/RamnagarMeals: Dinner

  2. Wake up early in the morning and proceed for an exciting Jungle Safari in Jim Corbett National Park.

    Established as India's first national park, Corbett is renowned for its diverse wildlife and beautiful forest landscapes. The park is home to Royal Bengal Tigers, Asian elephants, deer, leopards and a wide variety of bird species.

    Morning Jungle Safari

    Depending on permit availability, your safari may be arranged in one of the designated tourist zones, such as:

    • Bijrani
    • Jhirna
    • Dhela
    • Durgadevi
    • Dhikala
    • Garjiya
    • Sitabani
    • Sonanadi

    The safari zone, timing and vehicle are subject to forest department regulations and availability at the time of booking.

    Safari Vehicle Options

    Jeep/Gypsy Safari

    • Open 4×4 safari vehicle
    • Suitable for up to 6 guests
    • Ideal for families, couples and wildlife enthusiasts
    • Offers a more private safari experience

    Canter Safari

    • Open-roof shared safari vehicle
    • Suitable for larger groups
    • Generally, accommodates approximately 15–20 guests

    After completing the safari, return to the resort and enjoy breakfast.

    Afternoon – Corbett Museum

    After lunch, proceed towards Corbett Museum at Kaladhungi, located in the former residence of Jim Corbett.

    The historic heritage property houses memorabilia and personal belongings associated with Jim Corbett and provides an insight into his life and connection with the region.

    Spend some time exploring the museum and its surroundings before returning towards Corbett.

    Return to your resort in the evening and relax.

    Overnight Stay: Corbett/RamnagarMeals: Breakfast & Dinner

  3. After breakfast, proceed for a full-day excursion covering some of the scenic natural attractions around the Corbett region.

    Corbett Waterfall

    Begin the day's sightseeing with a visit to Corbett Waterfall, a picturesque natural attraction surrounded by dense forest.

    The waterfall is located approximately 25 km from Ramnagar and drops from a height of around 20 feet. Enjoy a peaceful walk through the forested surroundings and spend some time appreciating the natural beauty of the area.

    You may also enjoy photography and a short nature walk around the waterfall.

    Sitabani Forest

    Continue towards the Sitabani Forest area, known for its peaceful surroundings, forest landscapes and birdwatching opportunities.

    Unlike the core tourist zones of Corbett Tiger Reserve, Sitabani lies outside the Corbett Tiger Reserve, offering a different type of nature and forest experience.

    Enjoy:

    • Scenic forest drive
    • Birdwatching
    • Nature photography
    • Beautiful forest landscapes
    • Visit to Sitabani Temple, subject to local access

    Kosi River & Dhikuli

    Later, proceed towards the Kosi River and Dhikuli area, known for its scenic surroundings and peaceful atmosphere.

    Spend some time enjoying the views of the river, surrounding forests and nearby landscapes. The area is also suitable for birdwatching and nature photography.

    After completing the sightseeing, return to your resort in the evening.

    Overnight Stay: Corbett/RamnagarMeals: Breakfast & Dinner

  4. After breakfast, check out from your resort and begin your return journey towards Delhi.

    Before departure, enjoy some leisure time at the resort, or explore the nearby area for last-minute photographs and souvenir shopping, depending on your departure schedule.

    Upon arrival in Delhi, proceed to your preferred hotel, residence, railway station or airport for your onward journey.

    Meals: Breakfast

    End of Tour
