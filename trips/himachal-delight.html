Day by day

  1. Distance: Approx. 340 kmTravel Time: 8–9 hours

    • Stay: Shimla

    Start your journey with an early morning pickup from Delhi, driving north via Panipat, Chandigarh, Kalka, and Solan. Enjoy scenic views of the Shivalik Hills along the route. Arrive in Shimla by evening, check into your hotel, and unwind. Spend your evening at leisure exploring the nearby market, depending on your arrival time.

    • Stay: Shimla

    After breakfast, head out for a full-day excursion to Kufri (about 20 km from Shimla), visiting the Himalayan Nature Park, Green Valley, Wildflower Hall viewpoint, Fagu, and optional adventure activities at Kufri Fun World. Return to Shimla to explore the iconic Jakhoo Temple, Mall Road, The Ridge, Christ Church, and Scandal Point. Conclude your day with free time for shopping and leisure along Mall Road.

  3. Distance: Approx. 250–270 kmTravel Time: 8–10 hours

    • Stay: Manali

    Enjoy breakfast and check out from your hotel as you drive toward Manali through the beautiful Himalayan valleys. Make photography and sightseeing stops along the way at Sundernagar, Mandi, Pandoh Dam, the Kullu Valley, and local river viewpoints, with an optional stop at a Kullu Shawl Factory. Arrive in Manali, check in, and spend a relaxing evening around Mall Road or Old Manali.

    • Stay: Manali

    Following breakfast, embark on a full-day excursion toward the Solang and Lahaul valleys. Your journey covers Nehru Kund, Solang Valley (with optional adventure activities), the Atal Tunnel, and Sissu to view the waterfall, lake area, and valley viewpoints.

    Note: Sissu is accessed via the Atal Tunnel (approx. 13 km from Manali), and route availability depends on weather conditions.

    Return to Manali by evening for an overnight stay.

    • Stay: Dharamshala

    This is one of the longest days of the itinerary, so an early start is recommended. After breakfast and check-out, drive alongside the Parvati River to explore the Kasol market and riverside area.

    Continue toward Manikaran to visit the Manikaran Sahib Gurudwara, natural hot springs, Ram Temple, and the Parvati River bank. Afterward, journey onward to Dharamshala, arriving by evening or night to check in and relax.

    • Stay: Dharamshala

    After breakfast, explore the seamless sightseeing circuit connecting Dharamshala to the pine- and cedar-forested upper area of McLeod Ganj. Key stops include the Dalai Lama Temple Complex, Bhagsunag Temple, Bhagsu Waterfall, Tibetan Market, St. John in the Wilderness Church, Naddi View Point, Dal Lake, the Dharamshala Cricket Stadium (subject to access), and local tea gardens. Spend your evening café hopping and shopping in McLeod Ganj before returning to the hotel.

  7. Distance: Approx. 240–250 kmTravel Time: 6–8 hours (traffic dependent)

    • Stay: Chandigarh

    Check out after breakfast and begin your drive toward Chandigarh. En-route, you can take in Kangra Valley viewpoints, visit the historic Kangra Fort (time permitting), and stop by the Pong Dam or Palampur tea region depending on your exact route and timing. Arrive in Chandigarh, check into your hotel, and spend a quiet evening at leisure.

  8. After breakfast and check-out, discover Chandigarh's top urban landmarks including the Rock Garden, serene Sukhna Lake, Rose Garden, the Capitol Complex (outside view), and the vibrant Sector 17 Plaza. Following your tour, commence the drive back to Delhi, arriving by evening or night to conclude your Himalayan holiday with wonderful memories.
