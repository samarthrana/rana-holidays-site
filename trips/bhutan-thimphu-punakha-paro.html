Bhutan: Thimphu, Punakha & Paro

Dochula Pass, Punakha Dzong, Paro valley

  • 5N / 6D
  • Travel style: Couple
  • 3 hotel stays
  1. Delhi ✈start
  2. Phuentsholing1N
  3. Thimphu2N
  4. Paro1N
  5. Phuentsholing1N
DaysStaysIncludedPrice

Day by day

  1. Upon arrival at Bagdogra Airport (IXB), meet your driver and proceed towards Phuentsholing, the gateway to Bhutan.

    Complete the immigration formalities and proceed to your hotel. Check-in and relax.

    Dinner & Overnight Stay at Hotel in Phuentsholing.

    Meals: DinnerAccommodation: Hotel in Phuentsholing

  2. After breakfast at the hotel, check out and proceed towards Thimphu, the capital city of Bhutan.

    Enjoy the scenic drive through the beautiful Bhutanese mountains and valleys.

    Upon arrival in Thimphu, check in to the hotel and relax.

    Dinner & Overnight Stay at Hotel in Thimphu.

    Meals: Breakfast & DinnerAccommodation: Hotel in Thimphu

  3. After breakfast at the hotel, proceed for a full-day excursion to Dochula Pass and Punakha.

    Visit:

    • Dochula Pass & 108 Druk Wangyal Chortens
    • Druk Wangyal Lhakhang
    • Chimi Lhakhang
    • Punakha Dzong
    • Punakha Suspension Bridge

    Dochula Pass offers beautiful Himalayan views and is located on the scenic route between Thimphu and Punakha.

    After sightseeing, drive back to Thimphu.

    Dinner & Overnight Stay at Hotel in Thimphu.

    Meals: Breakfast & DinnerAccommodation: Hotel in Thimphu

  4. After breakfast at the hotel, check out and proceed towards Paro.

    Before leaving Thimphu, visit:

    • Buddha Dordenma
    • Memorial Chorten
    • Tashichho Dzong
    • Takin Preserve

    Later, drive towards Paro. On arrival, visit Rinpung Dzong and explore Paro Town.

    Check in to your hotel and relax.

    Dinner & Overnight Stay at Hotel in Paro.

    Meals: Breakfast & DinnerAccommodation: Hotel in Paro

  5. After breakfast at the hotel, check out and proceed towards Phuentsholing.

    Depending on the available time, enjoy local sightseeing in Paro before departure, such as:

    • Kyichu Lhakhang
    • Paro Town
    • Local Handicraft Shops

    Continue your scenic drive towards Phuentsholing.

    On arrival, check in to the hotel and relax.

    Dinner & Overnight Stay at Hotel in Phuentsholing.

    Meals: Breakfast & DinnerAccommodation: Hotel in Phuentsholing

  6. After breakfast at the hotel, check out and proceed towards Bagdogra Airport (IXB) for your onward journey.

    Tour Ends with Sweet Memories of Bhutan.

    Meals: Breakfast
