Day by day

  1. Meals: No MealAccommodation: Hotel in Hanoi

    Arrive at Noi Bai International Airport, where you will be warmly welcomed by our local guide and transferred to your hotel.

    The transfer from the airport to central Hanoi takes approximately 40 minutes, depending on traffic.

    Hanoi, also known as the “City of Rivers,” is the capital of Vietnam and boasts a history of more than a thousand years. The city combines ancient cultural heritage with elegant French colonial architecture, tree-lined boulevards, charming lakes, traditional houses, and vibrant local streets.

    Upon arrival at the hotel, complete the check-in formalities and spend the remainder of the day at leisure.

  2. Meals: BreakfastAccommodation: Hotel in Hanoi

    After breakfast, begin a full-day sightseeing tour of Hanoi.

    Ho Chi Minh Complex

    Start your day with a visit to the Ho Chi Minh Complex, dedicated to the life and legacy of Ho Chi Minh, the founder of modern Vietnam.

    Visit:

    • Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum
    • Presidential Palace
    • One Pillar Pagoda

    Continue to the Temple of Literature, one of Vietnam's most important historical and cultural landmarks. Built in 1070 during the Ly Dynasty, it was established to honor Confucius and later became Vietnam's first university.

    Hanoi Museum & Hoan Kiem Lake

    In the afternoon, visit one museum of your choice, depending on the museum's operating schedule:

    • Vietnam Museum of Fine Arts
    • National Museum of Vietnamese History
    • Museum of Revolution
    • Vietnam Military History Museum
    • Vietnam Museum of Ethnology

    Later, visit Hoan Kiem Lake, located in the heart of Hanoi. The lake is closely associated with the famous legend of the sacred sword and the giant turtle.

    End the day with a leisurely walk or shopping experience in Hanoi's famous Old Quarter, known for its historic streets, traditional shops, cafés, local restaurants, and lively atmosphere.

  3. Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & DinnerAccommodation: Overnight Cruise in Ha Long Bay

    After breakfast, depart from your hotel at approximately 08:00 hrs for Ha Long Bay, located around 160 km from Hanoi.

    The journey takes approximately 3.5 hours. Along the way, enjoy views of the countryside and the fertile Red River Delta, with opportunities to observe traditional Vietnamese villages and farmers working in the rice fields.

    Make a short stop en route at a local handicraft workshop before continuing to Ha Long.

    Arrive in Ha Long City at approximately 11:30 hrs and board your cruise. After a welcome drink and check-in, begin cruising through the spectacular waters of Ha Long Bay.

    Enjoy lunch on board while taking in the breathtaking scenery of limestone islands and emerald-green waters.

    Ha Long Bay, meaning “Descending Dragon Bay,” is renowned for its spectacular karst formations, islands, and islets rising dramatically from the sea.

    During the cruise, enjoy views of famous landmarks such as:

    • Dinh Huong Island
    • Ga Choi Island
    • Dog Island
    • Sail Island

    Continue with a visit to Sung Sot Cave, one of the most impressive caves in Ha Long Bay, followed by a swimming stop at Titov Beach, subject to weather and sea conditions.

    Later, relax on board and enjoy the spectacular sunset over the bay.

    After dinner, enjoy an evening of leisure and try your hand at night squid fishing, usually around 21:00 hrs, depending on cruise arrangements.

    Spend the night aboard the cruise surrounded by the peaceful scenery of Ha Long Bay.

  4. Meals: Breakfast & Brunch/LunchAccommodation: Hotel in Ho Chi Minh City

    Wake up early to enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of Ha Long Bay at sunrise. Take in the fresh sea breeze and magnificent views as the bay gradually comes to life.

    After breakfast, continue cruising and visit the Human Head-shaped Island and Tortoise Island.

    Continue toward Bai Tu Long Bay, an equally beautiful area known for its spectacular limestone formations and peaceful surroundings.

    Return to the cruise vessel and enjoy brunch or lunch while cruising back toward the harbor.

    Disembark from the cruise and transfer back to Hanoi Airport for your flight to Ho Chi Minh City.

    Upon arrival in Ho Chi Minh City, you will be welcomed by our local guide and transferred to your hotel for check-in.

    Ho Chi Minh City

    Formerly known as Saigon, Ho Chi Minh City is Vietnam's largest city and an important economic and commercial center. The city offers a fascinating blend of modern skyscrapers, bustling markets, French colonial architecture, historic landmarks, and vibrant local life.

    Spend the evening at leisure.

  5. Meals: BreakfastAccommodation: Hotel in Ho Chi Minh City

    After breakfast, depart for the Cu Chi Tunnels, located approximately 75 km northwest of central Ho Chi Minh City.

    The Cu Chi Tunnels played an important role as an underground base and resistance network during the conflicts involving French and American forces.

    Explore the remarkable underground tunnel system, which included interconnected passageways, meeting rooms, kitchens, storage areas, clinics, wells, and emergency exits. The tunnels provide an insight into the difficult conditions and ingenious methods used by the people who lived and operated within this network.

    After the visit, return to Ho Chi Minh City.

    Ho Chi Minh City Sightseeing

    In the afternoon, continue with a city sightseeing tour.

    Visit the exterior of Saigon Notre-Dame Cathedral Basilica, a famous French colonial landmark currently subject to restoration work.

    Continue to the Saigon Central Post Office, an elegant colonial-era building designed and constructed in the late 19th and early 20th centuries.

    Next, visit the War Remnants Museum, which houses an extensive collection of photographs, documents, military equipment, and other exhibits related to the Vietnam War and earlier conflicts.

    End the day with free time for shopping and exploring Ben Thanh Market, one of the city's most famous markets.

  6. Meals: BreakfastAccommodation: Hotel in Ho Chi Minh City

    After breakfast, depart by road for My Tho, located approximately 65 km from Ho Chi Minh City. The journey takes around 2 hours, depending on traffic.

    The Mekong Delta is one of Vietnam's most fertile agricultural regions and is famous for its extensive network of rivers, canals, tropical orchards, and traditional villages.

    Upon arrival in My Tho, board a boat and cruise through the waterways while observing the local lifestyle and lush river scenery.

    Visit Unicorn Island, where you can stroll through tropical orchards, taste fresh seasonal fruits, and enjoy traditional Southern Vietnamese music.

    Continue to Ben Tre Province, famous for its coconut production. Visit a local workshop to see how traditional coconut candy is made and enjoy a short walk through the rural surroundings.

    Experience a traditional horse-drawn carriage ride before continuing your journey through the waterways.

    Visit a local bee-keeping household and enjoy fresh honey and traditional honey tea.

    After completing the Mekong Delta excursion, return by road to Ho Chi Minh City.

    The remainder of the evening is free at leisure.

  7. Meals: Breakfast

    Enjoy breakfast at the hotel and spend your remaining time at leisure until your scheduled transfer to the airport.

    At the appropriate time, transfer to Tan Son Nhat International Airport for your onward flight back home or to your next destination.

    End of Services.
